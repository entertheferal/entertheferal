How It Works

The Process

Join

Opt in to participate. Only members who join can vote or be nominated.

Suggest Ideas

Submit award category ideas like "Most likely to..." and upvote your favorites.

Categories Curated

Admins review suggestions, combine similar ones, and finalize the ballot.

Vote

Pick one person for each category. Your votes are completely anonymous.

Results Calculated

Votes are tallied and the cap algorithm is applied to spread the love.

Results Published

Winners are revealed with shareable cards for each category.

Fair Play

  • Anonymous voting. Nobody can see who voted for whom. Not even admins.
  • No self-votes. You can't vote for yourself in any category.
  • Spread the love. You can only vote for the same person 3 times per ballot.
  • Minimum votes. A category needs at least 2 votes to have a winner.

Everybody Gets a Turn

To keep things fun and prevent one person from winning everything, we use a gold cap.

The Cap

Each person can win at most 3 categories. If someone would win more, the next highest vote-getter takes gold instead.

Here's how it works:

  • Categories are processed in order of biggest margin (clearest wins first).
  • If the winner has already hit the cap, the runner-up wins.
  • If there's no eligible runner-up, the original winner is kept (marked specially).

Ties

When two or more people have the same vote count, they share the gold. Ties are celebrated, not broken.

Not Into It?

You can leave the poll anytime before voting starts. Once voting opens, you're locked in (so people can vote for you).

After results are published, you can opt out of having your name shared on social media cards.
