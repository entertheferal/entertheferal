how it works

one poll a month

    fair play

    the numbers this month

    nobody sees who voted for who.

    • not even admins. we only see totals, never who picked who.
    • your name is never saved next to your votes.
    • if you clear your browser you can't edit your ballot anymore, but your votes still count.
    • you can see that you voted. that's it.

    everybody gets a turn

    the golds rule

    nobody wins more than 3 golds in a month.

    if someone wins more than that, the extras go to whoever came next, so more people get a moment.

    we do the math the same way every time, biggest landslides first. nothing is picked by hand.

    example
    alexmost votes in 5
    alex keeps 3, the other 2 go to next in line
    next in linegot the 2 left over
    →
    ties share the gold.

    joining is your choice

    you have to opt in

    if you join

    • you can suggest ideas and vote
    • other members can vote for you
    • your name can show up in the results and on shared images
    • you can leave until joining closes

    if you don't

    • you stay off the list and out of the results
    • you can still read the results page
    • nobody is added automatically

    questions

    can i change my votes?

    yes, until voting closes. open your ballot and change any pick. skip any award you want. a partial ballot still counts.

    can i vote for myself?

    no. your name shows up greyed out on your own ballot.

    can i pick the same person for everything?

    you can pick the same person up to 3 times on your ballot. after that their name is greyed out and you have to pick someone else.

    what if an award only gets a couple of votes?

    an award needs at least 2 votes. if it doesn't get them, nobody wins it that month.

    who picks the awards?

    you do. everyone who joined can send in ideas, and we pick the final list.

    what happens to my info?

    your votes are anonymous and your name is only shown in results if you joined. you can delete your account any time in settings. read the privacy policy for the details.
