Community Guidelines

The Feral Collective is invite-only for a reason: we want to build a space where people actually want to show up. These guidelines help us do that.

Be a decent human

Treat people the way you'd want to be treated. Disagreement is fine; disrespect isn't. We're all here because someone vouched for us. Let's make them look good.

Keep it safe

  • No harassment. Don't target, threaten, or repeatedly bother anyone.
  • No hate. Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, and other forms of discrimination have no place here.
  • No doxxing. Don't share anyone's private information without their consent.
  • No spam. Don't flood the community with promotions, scams, or repetitive content.

Respect privacy

  • Don't screenshot or share private conversations, game night moments, or workout sessions outside the community without everyone's OK.
  • What happens in the pack stays in the pack.

Invite codes

Your invite code is personal. When you share it, you're vouching for that person. Don't post it publicly or give it to strangers. If someone you invited causes problems, we might have a conversation with you too.

Profile pictures

Your avatar should be:

  • You, an icon you like, or art you made
  • Not someone else's photo without permission
  • Not hateful imagery or symbols
  • Not explicit content

If something's wrong

If you see behavior that violates these guidelines, or if someone is making you uncomfortable:

Email [PRIVACY_EMAIL] and we'll handle it. You can also reach out to any admin directly.

Consequences

Depending on the situation, we may:

  • Have a conversation with you
  • Remove your avatar or bio
  • Temporarily limit your access
  • Deactivate your account

We're not trying to be cops. We're trying to keep this a place people want to be.

The short version

Be kind. Be real. Don't be a jerk. If you wouldn't do it at a friend's house party, don't do it here.
